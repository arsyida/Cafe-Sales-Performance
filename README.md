This project presents an end-to-end retail F&B sales analysis covering 9,514 transactions throughout 2023 (Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2023). The raw dataset from Kaggle was cleaned and standardized in Google Sheets using lookup and logical formulas, then visualized as an interactive dashboard in Looker Studio to uncover revenue trends, product demand, daily traffic patterns, and customer payment preferences.

## Key Questions Explored
1. **Overall Sales Performance**: Determining the baseline annual revenue, total transaction volume, and overall Average Order Value throughout 2023.
2. **Monthly Sales Trend**: Tracking month-over-month revenue performance from January to December 2023 to evaluate revenue stability.
3. **Top Products & Sales Volume**: Identifying best-selling menu items by unit volume across beverages and snacks.
4. **Sales by Day of Week**: Analyzing daily total customer spending from Sunday to Saturday to identify weekday versus weekend revenue patterns.
5. **Sales by Order Location**: Examining transaction distribution across location to understand customer ordering preferences.
6. **Sales by Payment Method**: Evaluating customer payment adoption across Digital Wallet, Cash, Credit Card, and Unknown transactions.

## Summary of Findings

Here are some key findings derived from the cafe sales data analysis:

* **Overall Revenue & AOV:** The cafe generated $84,868.50 in total revenue across 9,514 transactions, achieving an Average Order Value of $8.92.
* **Stable Monthly Trend:** Monthly revenue remained remarkably consistent throughout 2023, fluctuating within a narrow range of $6.6K to $7.3K.
* **Top-Selling Menu Items:** Coffee leads sales volume, closely followed by Salad, Tea, Cookie, and Juice.
* **Balanced Weekly Traffic:** Unlike typical cafes with sharp weekend spikes, daily spending is evenly distributed across the week between $11.6K and $12.4K.
* **Order Location:** Recorded orders are nearly evenly split between In-Store and Takeaway, while 39.6% of transactions lack location records (`Unknown`).
* **Payment Preferences:** Digital Wallet, Cash, and Credit Card hold virtually identical shares among identified payments, with 31.0% recorded as `Unknown`.
